BS CS · Subject 4

Discrete Structures (3+0)

The mathematical foundations of computer science — logic, proofs, sets, relations, functions, counting, graphs and trees. English with Roman Urdu support.

Home  /  BS CS  /  Discrete Structures

English. Discrete Structures introduces the mathematical foundations that underlie computer science: logic, proof techniques, sets, relations, functions, sequences and recurrences, number theory, counting, graphs and trees. Students learn to reason rigorously about software, algorithms and data, and they see how each structure appears in data structures, algorithms, databases, artificial intelligence and cryptography.

Roman Urdu. Discrete Structures un riyazi (mathematical) buniyadon ka course hai jin par computer science khari hai: logic, proof ke tareeqe, sets, relations, functions, sequences aur recurrences, number theory, counting, graphs aur trees. Is course mein talib-e-ilm software, algorithms aur data ke bare mein sakht (rigorous) andaz mein sochna seekhte hain, aur dekhte hain ke har structure data structures, algorithms, databases, artificial intelligence aur cryptography mein kahan istemal hota hai.

Course learning outcomes (CLOs)

CLO Learning outcome Roman Urdu Bloom
CLO-1 Understand the key concepts of Discrete Structures such as Sets, Permutations, Relations, Graphs and Trees etc. Discrete Structures ke bunyadi concepts, jaise Sets, Permutations, Relations, Graphs aur Trees wagera ko samajhna. C2
CLO-2 Apply formal logic proofs and/or informal, but rigorous, logical reasoning to real problems, such as predicting the behavior of software or solving problems such as puzzles. Formal logic proofs aur/ya ghair-rasmi magar sakht (rigorous) mantiqi istadlal ko haqiqi masail par lagoo karna, jaise software ke behavior ki peshgoi ya puzzles hal karna. C3
CLO-3 Apply discrete structures into other computing problems such as formal specification, verification, databases, artificial intelligence, and cryptography. Discrete structures ko doosre computing masail mein istemal karna, jaise formal specification, verification, databases, artificial intelligence aur cryptography. C3
CLO-4 Differentiate various discrete structures and their relevance within the context of computer science, in the areas of data structures and algorithms, in particular. Mukhtalif discrete structures mein farq karna aur computer science mein, khaas kar data structures aur algorithms ke tanazur mein, unki ahmiyat bayan karna. C4
