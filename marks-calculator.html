Frequently asked questions

How is percentage calculated?

Percentage = (marks obtained ÷ total marks) × 100. The calculator does this for you.

Does it show pass or fail?

Yes. It shows pass or fail for each subject you enter.

Can I use it for any class?

Yes. Enter your own subjects and total marks.
