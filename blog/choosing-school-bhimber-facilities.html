Choosing a School in Bhimber: Facilities, Distance and Class Size

A good school is more than a good name. Here are the practical things to check, backed by national data.

Home  /  Blog  /  Choosing a School in Bhimber: Facilities, Distance and Class Size

By Startwell Education · 2026-11-13

What the national data shows

In 2021–22, 70% of schools in Pakistan had electricity, 79% had toilets, 79% had boundary walls and 78% had drinking water. The report notes large gaps between regions.

A look at AJK

The report’s charts for government schools show that in AJK only about 21% of primary schools had electricity and about 31% had drinking water, far lower than at higher levels. These charts do not cover private schools, so always inspect a school yourself.

Newer data: ASER 2025 surveyed rural AJK schools and found electricity in 84% of government and 93% of private schools. These are different surveys, so do not compare them directly. See government vs private schools in AJK.

Distance matters

UNESCO’s 2026 report cites Pakistan research where girls who travelled 45 to 60 minutes were 15 percentage points less likely to attend than those within 15 minutes. Consider travel time and transport.

Visit checklist

  1. Electricity, fans or heating, and lighting in classrooms
  2. Clean drinking water and separate, clean toilets
  3. Boundary wall and safe entry
  4. Class size and teacher qualifications
  5. Travel time and transport options
  6. Fees, progress reports and parent meetings
