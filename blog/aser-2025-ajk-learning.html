How Well Do Children Learn in Rural AJK? ASER 2025 Findings

ASER 2025 includes a section on rural Azad Jammu & Kashmir. Here is what it shows by class.

Home  /  Blog  /  How Well Do Children Learn in Rural AJK? ASER 2025 Findings

By Startwell Education · 2026-11-13

Percent of children who reached each level

ClassRead an Urdu storyRead English sentencesDo 2-digit division
121.9%14.7%17.7%
347.1%45.1%33.8%
578.6%77.0%68.0%

What stands out

  • Learning rises steadily through the grades. By Class 8, almost all children in the sample read a story in Urdu.
  • In Class 3, fewer than half read a story or English sentences, and only about a third do division.
  • Maths is the weakest area at every class shown.

Compared with Pakistan

Nationally, 54% of Class 5 children read English sentences and 48% do division. The rural AJK figures (77% and 68%) are higher, but the national figures include urban areas and the AJK sample is smaller, so compare with care.

What parents can do

  • Have your child read aloud and do simple sums at home.
  • Ask the school how it checks reading and maths in Classes 1 to 3.
  • Look at our primary programme and ask for progress reports.

Also read the national picture in ASER Pakistan 2025.

ASER 2025 is a provisional household survey. It samples children and schools, so treat results as estimates.
