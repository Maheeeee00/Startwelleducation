Private Schools in Pakistan: Why Nearly Half of Students Attend Them

Private schools are a major part of education in Pakistan. Here is what the statistics show and how to judge one.

Home  /  Blog  /  Private Schools in Pakistan: Why Nearly Half of Students Attend Them

By Startwell Education · 2026-11-13

The numbers

In 2021–22, 137,234 institutions (43.8%) were private. They enrolled 25,511,588 students, or 46.5% of all students, and employed 58% of teachers (1,249,746). UNESCO’s 2026 report puts the private share of enrolment in Pakistan at 47% at both primary and secondary level in 2024.

Public support for private schooling

UNESCO notes that Punjab’s Foundation Assisted Schools programme, which combines grants to schools and vouchers to households, increased enrolment.

What to check in any private school

  1. Teachers: qualifications and training.
  2. Class size: the national pupil-teacher ratio is 39 in primary.
  3. Facilities: clean water, toilets, safe building.
  4. Fees: ask for the full written fee list.
  5. Communication: regular progress reports and parent meetings.
