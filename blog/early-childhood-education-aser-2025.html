Early Childhood Education in Pakistan and AJK: What Parents Should Know

The years from 3 to 5 prepare children for school. Here is how many attend, and what is on offer.

Home  /  Blog  /  Early Childhood Education in Pakistan and AJK: What Parents Should Know

By Startwell Education · 2026-11-13

How many children attend?

ASER 2025 found that 59% of children aged 3–5 were enrolled in early childhood education, while 41% were not. Pakistan Education Statistics 2021–22 put the pre-primary gross enrolment ratio at 71%, down from 84% in 2016–17.

What AJK schools have

In rural AJK, 20.5% of surveyed government schools and 28.9% of private schools had early childhood education equipment.

Why it matters

UNESCO’s 2026 report summarises studies showing that structured early learning improves thinking, social and emotional skills and eases the move to primary school.

What to look for

  • Play-based, hands-on learning
  • Early phonics and numbers
  • Trained, warm teachers and small groups
  • Child-safe space and toys

See our Early Years programme, and read why early years matters.

ASER 2025 is a provisional household survey. It samples children and schools, so treat results as estimates.
