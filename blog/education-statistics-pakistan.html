Education in Pakistan in Numbers: What the Latest Data Shows

How big is Pakistan’s education system, and where does it struggle? Here are the key figures for parents, using two major reports.

Home  /  Blog  /  Education in Pakistan in Numbers: What the Latest Data Shows

By Startwell Education · 2026-11-13

The size of the system

The Pakistan Education Statistics 2021–22 report counts 313,418 educational institutions, of which 227,506 (73%) are formal schools from primary to higher secondary. There are 54,870,964 enrolled students and 2,139,631 teachers.

Public and private

About 56.2% of institutions are public and 43.8% private. Students are split 53.5% public and 46.5% private, and private schools employ 58% of teachers. See private schools in Pakistan.

Enrolment drops at higher levels

The gross enrolment ratio (GER) was 76% for primary, 51% for lower secondary and 32% for upper secondary in 2021–22. Primary GER fell from 97% in 2016–17 to 76%.

Staying in school

The survival rate to grade 5 improved from 67% to 77%. The effective transition rate was 81% from primary to middle and 90% from middle to high.

Class size

The pupil-teacher ratio was 39 in primary, 25 in middle and 30 in upper secondary, higher than in 2016–17.

The global picture

UNESCO’s 2026 report says 273 million children, adolescents and youth were out of school worldwide in 2024, about one in six, even though 1.4 billion students were enrolled. Read more on out-of-school children in Pakistan.

What this means for parents

Strong numbers nationally do not guarantee a good school locally. Ask about class size, teacher qualifications and regular progress reports. See how to choose a school in Bhimber.
