StartWell Spelling Bee

Spell it right, spell it bright!

The StartWell Spelling Bee is our school-wide English spelling competition. It builds vocabulary, pronunciation, listening skills and the confidence to perform on stage.

  • Open to students of all classes
  • Age-appropriate word lists for each category
  • Certificates for all finalists, trophies for winners

Junior

Class 1 – 3

Middle

Class 4 – 6

Senior

Class 7 – 10

Champions

One winner per category

Competition Format

Three rounds to the trophy

ROUND 1

Class Round

Every student takes part in a written spelling test in their own classroom. Top spellers move ahead.

ROUND 2

Section Round

Class winners compete orally within their category — one word at a time.

GRAND FINAL

On Stage

Finalists spell on stage in front of judges, parents and the whole school.

Rules

How the Bee works

  • The pronouncer says the word, uses it in a sentence and repeats it.
  • Spellers may ask for the meaning or the word to be repeated.
  • Say the word, spell it, then say it again.
  • Once a letter is said, it cannot be changed.
  • The judges' decision is final.
Preparation Tips

Get ready to spell

Read Daily

Reading every day builds natural spelling.

Use Word Lists

Practise the list given by your teacher.

Learn Roots

Prefixes and suffixes unlock new words.

Practise Aloud

Spell out loud with family and friends.
