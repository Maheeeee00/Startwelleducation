              Step-by-step guides

              Turn photos of my homework into one PDF
              1. Take clear photos of each page in good light, with the page flat.
              2. Click Images → PDF above and choose all your photos.
              3. Use the ↑ ↓ arrows to put the pages in the right order.
              4. Click Make PDF & download.
              5. Open the PDF and check every page before you hand it in.
              Combine several PDFs into one file
              1. Click Merge PDFs and choose all the files.
              2. Put them in order with the arrows.
              3. Click Merge & download.
              Fix a page that is sideways or upside down
              1. Click Split, rotate & delete pages and choose your PDF.
              2. Set What to do to Rotate these pages.
              3. Type the page number (for example 3) and choose how far to turn it.
              4. Click Apply & download.
              Hand in only some pages of a PDF
              1. Click Split, rotate & delete pages and choose your PDF.
              2. Choose Keep only these pages and type the pages, e.g. 2-4, 7.
              3. Click Apply & download.

              Good to know

              • Your files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
              • Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked before you can change them.
              • Always open and check your downloaded file before handing it in.
              • Using a tool to change a file does not change who did the work — hand in your own work.

              Built with the open-source pdf-lib (MIT) and pdf.js (Apache-2.0) libraries.
