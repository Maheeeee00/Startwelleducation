Requirements

6. Required hardware

  • Computer with webcam and headset
  • Smartphone (optional)

7. Required software

  • Gmail (or Outlook)
  • Google Calendar
  • Google Meet (or Teams/Zoom)
  • Google Docs

8. Learning objectives

  1. Compose and send formal emails using To, CC, BCC and attachments.
  2. Create a signature, labels and a filter.
  3. Schedule and join a video meeting with correct etiquette.

9. Background theory

Formal email follows a fixed structure and uses recipient fields correctly. Calendar invitations automatically attach meeting links and reminders.

Revise the detailed explanation in Lecture 4 before starting.

10. Step-by-step procedure

  1. Open Gmail and go to Settings → See all settings → Signature. Create a signature with name, programme, roll number and phone.
  2. Create two labels: ICT Course and Assignments.
  3. Write a leave application to the instructor (To) and a classmate (CC) with subject ‘Leave application – YourName – Date’.
  4. Attach a PDF (any sample file) and confirm it appears before sending.
  5. Send a group announcement to three classmates using BCC only, with your own address in To.
  6. Create a filter: emails from the instructor’s address are labelled ICT Course automatically (search → Show search options → Create filter).
  7. Open Google Calendar, create an event ‘ICT Study Group’, click ‘Add Google Meet video conferencing’ and invite two classmates.
  8. Join the meeting at the set time. Practise muting, turning the camera on and off, raising a hand, using chat and presenting your screen for one minute.
  9. Reply to the instructor’s test email using Reply (not Reply all).
  10. Take screenshots and paste them into Lab04_YourName with captions.

11. Practical task

Send three correctly structured emails (leave application with CC and attachment, BCC announcement, reply), set up a signature, labels and a filter, and host a 10-minute Meet session with screen sharing.

12. Expected output

Three sent emails visible in the Sent folder, a working filter and label, a calendar event with a Meet link and a lab document with screenshots.

14. Troubleshooting guide

ProblemSolution
Attachment too largeUse the Drive icon in the compose window to insert a link instead.
Email went to spamAsk the receiver to mark it ‘Not spam’ and avoid all-caps subjects.
Meet shows ‘camera blocked’Click the camera icon in Chrome’s address bar and allow access.
Echo in the meetingAll but the speaker should mute; use headphones.

15. Lab questions

  1. Why did you place your own address in To when using BCC?
  2. What does the filter you created do?
  3. Which meeting control did you find most useful?
  4. What is the size limit for Gmail attachments?

16. Viva questions

  1. Difference between Reply and Reply all?
  2. What is SMTP?
  3. Name two signs of a phishing email.
  4. Why should you mute in a meeting?
