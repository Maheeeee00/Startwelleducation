6. Required hardware

  • Computer with internet

7. Required software

  • Chrome or Edge
  • Google and Bing
  • Google Scholar
  • Word or Google Docs

8. Learning objectives

  1. Use six search operators correctly.
  2. Locate official and academic sources.
  3. Evaluate sources with the CRAAP test.
  4. Record citations.

9. Background theory

Search engines rank pages; operators instruct them precisely. Reliable research uses official, academic and well-referenced sources and records where each fact came from.

Revise the detailed explanation in Lecture 3 before starting.

10. Step-by-step procedure

  1. Create Lab03_YourName with a table: Challenge | Query used | Source URL | Answer.
  2. Challenge 1: Find an exact definition of “digital divide” using quotes.
  3. Challenge 2: Find an ICT course outline in PDF: ICT course outline filetype:pdf.
  4. Challenge 3: Find an HEC page about undergraduate policy using site:hec.gov.pk.
  5. Challenge 4: Search python programming -snake and note how results change.
  6. Challenge 5: Find either Matric OR SSC result pages for BISE Mirpur.
  7. Challenge 6: Use intitle:scholarship site:edu.pk.
  8. Challenge 7: Search Google Scholar for ‘ICT in education Pakistan’ and note one paper’s title, authors, year and citation count.
  9. Challenge 8: Run challenge 2 on Bing and note one difference.
  10. Challenge 9: Pick one blog and one official page on the same topic and complete a CRAAP table for each.
  11. Challenge 10: Write two references in a consistent style (author, title, site, year, URL).
  12. Save and upload to ICT-Labs.

11. Practical task

Complete all 10 search challenges, recording the exact query, source and answer, plus two CRAAP evaluations and two formatted references.

12. Expected output

Lab03_YourName with a completed 10-row table, two CRAAP tables and two references.

14. Troubleshooting guide

ProblemSolution
Operator seems ignoredMake sure there is no space after the colon: site:hec.gov.pk not site: hec.gov.pk.
Google shows a CAPTCHAToo many searches from the lab IP; wait a minute or switch to Bing. Do not use tools to bypass it.
Scholar result has no PDFTry the ‘All versions’ link or note the citation only.
Quotes return nothingThe exact phrase may not exist; remove one word and retry.

15. Lab questions

  1. Which operator was most useful, and why?
  2. How did Google and Bing results differ?
  3. Why did the blog score lower in CRAAP?
  4. How many citations did your Scholar paper have?

16. Viva questions

  1. What does the minus operator do?
  2. Explain crawling.
  3. Why is site:gov.pk useful?
  4. What does ‘Currency’ mean in CRAAP?
