8. Learning objectives

  1. Join a class and submit an assignment on an LMS.
  2. Participate in a forum and take a quiz.
  3. Plan an online course and start a professional profile.

9. Background theory

An LMS organises learning activities and records submissions and grades. MOOCs provide self-paced courses. Professional profiles showcase learning.

Revise the detailed explanation in Lecture 9 before starting.

10. Step-by-step procedure

  1. Join the instructor’s Google Classroom using the class code.
  2. Open the ‘Lab 09 Submission’ assignment and read the rubric.
  3. Post a question or comment in the Classroom Stream about any lecture topic and reply to one classmate.
  4. Take the ‘Lecture 9 Quiz’ (Google Form) attached to Classwork and note your score.
  5. Visit a Moodle demo site or your university LMS and identify where to find course files, assignments, forums and grades; record in a table.
  6. Choose a MOOC platform and find one free or audit course related to ICT or your major. Record title, provider, duration, workload and certificate cost.
  7. Plan three modules with dates in Google Calendar as reminders.
  8. Create or update a LinkedIn profile: photo, headline, education, three skills and the planned course.
  9. Write a one-page Lab09 report with the LMS feature table, course plan and profile screenshot.
  10. Submit the report through the Classroom assignment and click Turn in.

11. Practical task

Complete an end-to-end LMS workflow (join, discuss, quiz, submit), compare LMS features, plan a MOOC with calendar reminders and set up a LinkedIn profile.

12. Expected output

Turned-in Lab09 assignment on Classroom, quiz score, forum posts, calendar reminders and LinkedIn profile screenshot.

14. Troubleshooting guide

ProblemSolution
Class code not workingMake sure you are signed in with the correct Google account; school accounts may block outside classes.
Quiz form says you need permissionSign in with the account added to the class.
Turn in button missingYou may have already turned in; look for ‘Unsubmit’.
LinkedIn asks for phone verificationUse your own number or skip optional steps; never share OTPs with others.

15. Lab questions

  1. Which LMS feature did you find most useful?
  2. What did your quiz score show about your understanding?
  3. What course did you choose and why?
  4. What is your LinkedIn headline?

16. Viva questions

  1. Define LMS.
  2. What is the difference between auditing and a paid certificate?
  3. Give an example of asynchronous learning.
  4. Why is a professional profile useful for students?
