6. Required hardware

  • Computer

7. Required software

  • PowerPoint or Google Slides
  • Excel/Sheets for chart data

8. Learning objectives

  1. Build a five-slide presentation using layouts and a theme.
  2. Insert an image, a chart and a diagram.
  3. Add speaker notes and rehearse with Presenter View.

9. Background theory

Slides use layouts and themes for consistency. Visuals and short text support the speaker; notes hold the details.

Revise the detailed explanation in Lecture 7 before starting.

10. Step-by-step procedure

  1. Create a new presentation named Lab07_YourName and choose a simple theme with good contrast.
  2. Slide 1 (Title Slide): ‘Mera Shehar Bhimber’, your name and roll number.
  3. Open View → Slide Master and add a small footer text ‘ICT Lab 07’ that appears on all slides; close Master view.
  4. Slide 2 (Title and Content): Location and access with three short bullets and a map screenshot.
  5. Slide 3 (Two Content): History, with a photo of a heritage site (credited) and two bullets.
  6. Slide 4: insert a column chart (Insert → Chart) showing any four facts about Bhimber with sample or researched numbers and a takeaway title.
  7. Slide 5: use SmartArt/diagram to show ‘How to reach Bhimber from Mirpur’ in 3–4 steps.
  8. Slide 6: Summary with three key points and ‘Questions?’.
  9. Write 3–5 sentences of speaker notes for every slide.
  10. Apply one Fade transition to all slides; add an Appear animation to the SmartArt steps.
  11. Check every slide: font ≥ 28 pt for body, under 30 words, aligned elements.
  12. Start Presenter View, rehearse twice with the timer and note your time.
  13. Export as PDF and save both files to ICT-Labs.

11. Practical task

Create and rehearse a six-slide ‘Mera Shehar Bhimber’ presentation with theme, Slide Master footer, image, chart, diagram, notes, one transition and limited animation, delivered within two minutes.

12. Expected output

Lab07_YourName.pptx (or Slides link) and Lab07_YourName.pdf, plus your recorded rehearsal time.

14. Troubleshooting guide

ProblemSolution
Footer not showing on all slidesUse Insert → Header & Footer → ‘Apply to All’, or edit the master rather than one layout.
Chart editing window does not openExcel must be installed for desktop charts; use PowerPoint for the web or Slides with Sheets.
Presenter View shows on the projectorUse ‘Swap displays’ in the slide show toolbar.
Fonts change on another computerUse standard fonts or embed fonts in File → Options → Save.

15. Lab questions

  1. What was your rehearsal time?
  2. Which slide had the most effective visual?
  3. How did the Slide Master save time?
  4. How many words is your longest slide?

16. Viva questions

  1. Difference between a theme and a layout?
  2. What does Presenter View show?
  3. When is animation useful?
  4. Why use speaker notes?
