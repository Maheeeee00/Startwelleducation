Course description

Applications of ICT teaches the practical use of Information and Communication Technology and software tools in everyday, academic and professional life. Students learn the components and scope of ICT, use search engines, email and video conferencing, produce documents, spreadsheets and presentations, manage files in the cloud, learn through Learning Management Systems, use digital health, finance and shopping services safely, and practise responsible digital citizenship and ethics. Examples are drawn from Pakistan, Azad Jammu and Kashmir, Bhimber, university and school life.

Roman Urdu: Yeh course students ko ICT aur software tools ke practical applications sikhata hai, taake woh roz marra masail hal kar sakein, productivity barha sakein aur naye kaam kar sakein. Examples cricket, Bhimber Mart aur school life se hain.

Course objectives

  1. Build a clear understanding of ICT concepts, components and scope.
  2. Develop practical skills in widely used productivity tools: word processing, spreadsheets and presentations.
  3. Enable students to communicate, collaborate and learn effectively using digital platforms.
  4. Prepare students to use ICT safely in health, finance and online shopping.
  5. Develop ethical, legal and responsible behaviour as digital citizens.

Unit-wise syllabus

Unit 1: Introduction to ICT

Lecture 1: ICT Fundamentals: Hardware, Software, Networks and Storage
What is ICT?; Hardware: the physical parts; Software: the instructions; Networks: connecting systems; Storage: local and cloud

Lecture 2: Scope of ICT and Emerging Technologies
ICT in education; ICT in business; ICT in governance (e-governance); ICT in healthcare and digital media; Emerging technologies

Unit 2: Basic ICT Productivity Tools

Lecture 3: Search Engines and Web Research
How a search engine works; Choosing keywords; Search operators; Evaluating sources: the CRAAP test; Google, Bing and specialised tools

Lecture 4: Email and Formal Digital Communication
How email works; To, CC, BCC and attachments; Structure of a formal email; Email etiquette and safety; Video conferencing

Lecture 5: Microsoft Word and Google Docs
Word processors and formatting; Styles and headings; Page layout: numbers, headers, footers and breaks; Table of contents, tables and images; References, comments and collaboration

Lecture 6: Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets
Spreadsheet basics; Formulas and operators; Essential functions; Relative and absolute references; Sorting, filtering, formatting and charts

Lecture 7: Microsoft PowerPoint and Google Slides
Presentation software basics; Design principles; Visuals: images, charts and diagrams; Transitions and animations; Speaker notes and delivery

Lecture 8: Cloud Storage and Digital Note-Taking
Cloud storage services; Sync, version history and offline access; Sharing and permissions; Folder structure and file naming; Digital note-taking and backup

Unit 3: ICT in Education

Lecture 9: ICT in Education and Learning Management Systems
E-learning and its forms; Learning Management Systems; Online course platforms (MOOCs); Interactive multimedia and virtual classrooms; Professional profiles and online learning skills

Unit 4: Health, Finance and Shopping

Lecture 10: ICT in Health, Finance and Shopping
Fitness and health tracking; Telemedicine and digital health; Digital finance: wallets, banking and payments; E-commerce and safe online shopping; Digital fraud and protection

Unit 5: Digital Citizenship and Ethics

Lecture 11: Digital Citizenship and Netiquette
Digital identity and digital footprint; Online reputation and netiquette; Cyberbullying and online harassment; Privacy settings and account security; Laws and responsibilities: PECA 2016

Lecture 12: Digital Ethics, Copyright, Plagiarism, Misinformation and Final Project
Intellectual property and copyright; Fair use and Creative Commons; Plagiarism and how to avoid it; AI tools and academic integrity; Misinformation and fact-checking

Teaching methodology

  • Interactive lectures with real-life Pakistani and Bhimber examples
  • Live software demonstrations followed by guided practice
  • Weekly two-hour hands-on lab linked to each lecture
  • Think-pair-share, group mapping and role-play activities
  • LMS-based quizzes, forums and assignment submission
  • Project-based learning culminating in a Digital Portfolio
