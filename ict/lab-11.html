8. Learning objectives

  1. Audit your digital footprint.
  2. Configure privacy settings on two platforms.
  3. Create a strong password policy and enable 2SV.
  4. Practise responding to bullying scenarios.

9. Background theory

Footprints persist; privacy settings and strong authentication reduce exposure; netiquette and reporting reduce harm.

Revise the detailed explanation in Lecture 11 before starting.

10. Step-by-step procedure

  1. Search your full name and username in Google; note what appears on the first page (do not include others’ private information).
  2. Open one social media account. Record five privacy settings before and after you change them: post audience, profile visibility, phone-number search, tag review, location on posts.
  3. On WhatsApp: set profile photo and last seen to My contacts and restrict who can add you to groups.
  4. Open myaccount.google.com → Security. Turn on 2-Step Verification and review recent security activity and devices.
  5. Write your personal password rule (for example ‘four words + number + symbol, unique per site’). Do not write real passwords.
  6. Optionally check your email on haveibeenpwned.com and note whether it appeared in known breaches.
  7. Read three scenario cards provided by the instructor and write the correct response steps for each.
  8. Create a one-page netiquette poster with eight rules for a class group.
  9. Compile Lab11_YourName and upload.

11. Practical task

Complete a footprint audit, before/after privacy settings table for two platforms, enable 2SV, write a password rule, respond to three scenarios and design a netiquette poster.

12. Expected output

Lab11_YourName with audit summary, settings table, 2SV confirmation, scenario responses and poster.

14. Troubleshooting guide

ProblemSolution
2SV setup needs a phoneUse your own number or an authenticator app; ask the instructor if you have no phone.
Settings menu looks differentApps update often; search the setting name in the app’s settings search box.
Locked out after enabling 2SVUse the backup codes you saved.
Search results show another person with the same nameNote only results that are about you.

15. Lab questions

  1. What did you find in your name search?
  2. Which privacy setting change was most important?
  3. Why is 2SV important even with a strong password?
  4. What was your response to the fake-profile scenario?

16. Viva questions

  1. Define digital footprint.
  2. Name three netiquette rules.
  3. What is PECA 2016?
  4. What makes a password strong?
