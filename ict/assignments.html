Lecture-wise assignments

LectureAssignmentCLOs
L1Prepare a two-page report titled ‘The ICT system in my home’. List every ICT device in your home, classify each as input, processing, output, storage or communication, name the operating system and three applications used on each main device, and draw a simple diagram of how the devices connect to the internet.CLO 1
L2Choose one sector (education, business, governance, health or media). Write a three-page report on how ICT is used in that sector in Pakistan, including at least three real services or apps, one emerging technology that could improve it, and one risk with a suggested solution. Cite every source.CLO 1, CLO 2
L3Research question: ‘What is the current state of internet access in Azad Jammu and Kashmir?’ Find five sources using at least four different operators. For each source, write the query used, the URL, the date and a CRAAP evaluation. Conclude with a 300-word summary using only the sources you rated reliable.CLO 2, CLO 3
L4Write three formal emails in a document and send one of them to the instructor: (1) a leave application to your class advisor with CC to the coordinator, (2) an email requesting an internship from a local software house with your CV attached, (3) an email to all class members in BCC announcing a study group meeting with a Google Meet link and calendar invitation.CLO 2, CLO 3
L5Create a 3–4 page assignment on ‘Uses of ICT in my district’ with: title page without page number, automatic table of contents, at least three Heading 1 and two Heading 2 sections, one table, one image with caption, page numbers in the footer, header with course code, three citations with a bibliography, exported as PDF.CLO 3
L6Using the provided Bhimber Mart dataset (20 items), compute totals, overall SUM and AVERAGE, a Status column with IF, a discounted total using an absolute reference, COUNTIF of low-stock items, conditional formatting, a sorted top-five list and one correctly titled chart. Write a 150-word summary of findings for the shop owner.CLO 3
L7Prepare a 6–8 slide presentation on ‘ICT in my field of study’ using a consistent theme, one chart, one diagram, at least two relevant images with sources, speaker notes on every slide and no more than 30 words per slide. Present it in five minutes and submit the PPTX and a PDF.CLO 3
L8Design and implement a complete folder structure for your current semester in Google Drive or OneDrive, rename at least ten files using a convention, share one folder as Viewer with the instructor and create a OneNote or Keep notebook for ICT with notes from three lectures. Submit a one-page report with screenshots and your naming rule.CLO 2, CLO 3
L9Enrol in (or audit) one free online course related to your field on any MOOC platform. Complete at least two modules, then write a two-page review covering the platform, course structure, assessments, quality and how it compares with classroom learning. Add the course to a LinkedIn profile you create or update, and include screenshots.CLO 2, CLO 3
L10Prepare a ‘Digital Safety Guide for My Family’ (3 pages) covering: two telemedicine services and how to verify a doctor, how to use one mobile wallet safely, five rules for online shopping, five common frauds in Pakistan and what to do if money is stolen. Use simple English with Roman Urdu notes and cite sources.CLO 2, CLO 3
L11Conduct a digital footprint audit: search your name, review your social media profiles and list what is publicly visible. Then write a two-page report on changes you made (privacy settings, removed posts, 2SV enabled) and create a one-page netiquette poster for your class WhatsApp or LMS group.CLO 4
L12Final Project – Digital Portfolio: build a shared cloud folder containing (1) a one-page CV in Word/Docs, (2) a personal budget or Bhimber Mart analysis workbook in Excel/Sheets with formulas and a chart, (3) a five-slide presentation introducing yourself and your career goals with speaker notes, (4) an ICT reflection essay of 500 words with at least three citations, and (5) a README document listing files and sources. Share the folder link (Viewer) and present it in Week 15.CLO 4, CLO 3

Major projects

1Project 1: Academic Document Portfolio

IndividualAssigned Week 5, due Week 7CLO 3CLO 4

Produce three professional documents in Word or Google Docs that demonstrate formatting, structure and referencing skills.

Tasks

  1. A 4–5 page research report on an ICT topic with title page, automatic table of contents, Heading styles, page numbers, one table, one captioned figure and at least five citations with a bibliography.
  2. A formal application letter (for an internship or scholarship) using a proper letter layout.
  3. A one-page notice or flyer for a school event in Bhimber using tables or text boxes and an attributed image.
  4. Export all three as PDF and organise them in a cloud folder named ICT_P1_YourName.

Deliverables

  • ICT_P1_Report_YourName.pdf
  • ICT_P1_Letter_YourName.pdf
  • ICT_P1_Notice_YourName.pdf
  • Editable source files and a Viewer link

Marking rubric

CriterionMarks
Structure and styles25
Formatting and layout25
Content quality and originality30
Citations and attribution10
Organisation and submission10
Total100
2Project 2: Excel-Based Business Management System (Bhimber Mart)

Group of 2–3Assigned Week 6, due Week 10CLO 3

Build a small management workbook for Bhimber Mart using the sample dataset provided on the Resources page.

Tasks

  1. Sheet ‘Sales’: import the dataset, calculate line totals, discounted totals with an absolute discount cell and a Status column with IF for low stock.
  2. Sheet ‘Summary’: total, average, maximum and minimum sales; COUNTIF of low-stock items; SUMIF of sales by category.
  3. Sheet ‘Reorder’: filtered list of low-stock items with suggested reorder quantity (=20-StockLeft).
  4. Sheet ‘Dashboard’: a column chart of top 10 items and a pie chart of category share, with titles.
  5. Apply number formatting (Rs), conditional formatting, freeze panes and protect formula cells.
  6. Write a one-page memo to the owner with three recommendations based on the data.

Deliverables

  • ICT_P2_BhimberMart_GroupName.xlsx (or shared Sheet)
  • Owner memo (PDF)
  • Contribution statement of each member

Marking rubric

CriterionMarks
Formulas and functions30
Data tools (sort, filter, conditional format)20
Charts and dashboard20
Recommendations memo20
Teamwork and organisation10
Total100
3Project 3: Complete Digital Portfolio

IndividualLaunched Week 13, due Week 14, presented Week 15CLO 3CLO 4

Create an organised cloud portfolio that showcases your ICT skills, as described in Lecture 12 and Lab 12.

Tasks

  1. Professional one-page CV (Word/Docs, exported PDF).
  2. Excel workbook: personal budget or Bhimber Mart analysis with formulas and a chart.
  3. Five-slide PowerPoint/Slides presentation with speaker notes and an attributed CC image.
  4. 500-word reflection essay with three citations.
  5. README listing files, sources and licences.
  6. Organised cloud folder shared as Viewer with the instructor; three-minute presentation.

Deliverables

  • ICT_DigitalPortfolio_YourName folder link
  • README PDF on the LMS
  • Presentation in Week 15

Marking rubric

CriterionMarks
CV quality20
Spreadsheet20
Presentation and delivery20
Essay and citations20
Ethics, organisation and sharing20
Total100

Project 2 uses the Bhimber Mart sample dataset (CSV). Project 3 is the Final Project worth 20% of the course grade.

Submission instructions

  • Name files as Course_Item_YourName (e.g., ICT_A3_SanaAli.pdf).
  • Submit PDFs unless an editable file is requested.
  • Upload to the LMS before the deadline; late submissions lose 10% per day unless excused.
  • Keep a copy in your ICT cloud folder.
  • Cite every source; plagiarism is handled under the university’s policy.
