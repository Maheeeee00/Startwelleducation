Requirements

6. Required hardware

  • Smartphone
  • Computer with internet

7. Required software

  • Google Fit, Samsung Health or phone health app
  • A telemedicine website (Oladoc or Marham)
  • Daraz (or another e-commerce site)
  • A mobile wallet or bank app (demo screens only)

8. Learning objectives

  1. Record and interpret basic fitness data.
  2. Explore a telemedicine platform and verify a doctor profile.
  3. Evaluate e-commerce listings and identify fake-seller signs.
  4. Practise fraud recognition.

9. Background theory

Health apps estimate activity using sensors; telemedicine platforms list verified doctors; e-commerce safety depends on seller evaluation and secure payment.

Revise the detailed explanation in Lecture 10 before starting.

10. Step-by-step procedure

  1. Open a health app on your phone and note today’s steps, distance and active minutes; set a daily goal.
  2. Open Oladoc or Marham. Search for a general physician in Mirpur or Islamabad. Record the doctor’s qualification, PM&DC number if shown, fee and online consultation option. Do not book.
  3. Open Daraz and choose one product (for example a USB flash drive). Compare three listings by price, seller rating, reviews, warranty and return policy in a table.
  4. Add the best option to the cart and proceed to the payment options page only. Note available methods. Do not place the order or enter any payment details.
  5. Identify two fake-seller warning signs from social media advertisements shown by the instructor.
  6. Read three sample messages provided by the instructor and classify each as Safe or Fraud with reasons.
  7. Write a one-paragraph safe-payment checklist.
  8. Compile everything in Lab10_YourName and upload.

11. Practical task

Produce a lab report with fitness data and goal, a verified doctor profile summary, a three-listing comparison table with final choice, payment options observed (no purchase), fraud classification of three messages and a safe-payment checklist.

12. Expected output

Lab10_YourName report with tables, screenshots and checklist.

14. Troubleshooting guide

ProblemSolution
Health app shows zero stepsEnable physical activity permission in phone settings.
Telemedicine site asks to log inBrowse doctor listings without logging in; skip booking.
Accidentally placed an orderCancel immediately from ‘My Orders’; COD orders can usually be cancelled before shipping.
App store shows several similar wallet appsInstall only the official app by the verified publisher.

15. Lab questions

  1. How many steps did you record, and is it above your goal?
  2. How did you verify the doctor?
  3. Which listing did you choose and why?
  4. Which sample message was fraud, and how did you know?

16. Viva questions

  1. What is telemedicine?
  2. Name two Pakistani mobile wallets.
  3. Why is COD safer with unknown sellers?
  4. What should you do if someone asks for your OTP?
