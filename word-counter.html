Frequently asked questions

Does the word counter work with Urdu?

Yes. It counts Urdu words and characters as well as English.

Can I set a word limit?

Yes. Set a limit and the counter shows how close you are.

Is my text saved?

Only in your own browser, so it is still there if you refresh. It is never uploaded. Press the Clear button to remove it.
