Admission Process at Startwell Education

Admissions open for 2026–27 — Playgroup to Class 10 and Hifz.

Home  /  Admission Process

Admissions are open for session 2026–27, from Playgroup to Class 10, including our Hifz Department. Here is how to apply.

How to apply

  1. Call or visit. Call +92 340 1501700 or visit the school, Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM.
  2. Meet our teachers. Tour the campus and ask any questions about classes and teaching.
  3. Send your application. Fill in the online form on our Admissions page, or apply in person at the school.
  4. Complete admission at the school. Our office will guide you through the remaining steps. For fees and any entry assessment, please call us or ask at the campus. The documents needed are listed on our parent FAQ page.

Where to apply

Startwell Education, Bhimber, Azad Jammu & Kashmir, Pakistan — see on Google Maps. Office hours: Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

Classes available

Playgroup to Class 10 (Early Years, Primary, Middle and Secondary), and the Hifz Department.

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit before applying?

Yes. Parents are welcome to visit the campus and meet our teachers during school hours.

Which documents are needed?

Child’s B-Form, two passport-size photographs, a copy of the parent’s CNIC, and the school leaving certificate or last result card, if applicable.

Where can I find fee details?

Please call +92 340 1501700 or ask at the school office. We share up-to-date fees directly with parents.

Which campus should I choose?

Startwell Education has 4 campuses. See our campuses page or call us and we will help you choose the nearest one.
