FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When do admissions open?

Admissions are generally open at the start of each academic session. Mid-session admissions are available subject to seat availability.

What documents are required for admission?

Child's B-Form, two passport-size photographs, copy of parent's CNIC and the school leaving certificate / last result card (if applicable).

Can we visit the campus before applying?

Absolutely. Parents are welcome to visit during office hours to meet the staff and see the facilities.

Is transport available?

Please contact the school office for details about transport routes and availability.
