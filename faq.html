Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for parents about admissions and life at Startwell Education, Bhimber.

Home  /  FAQ

Admissions

Are admissions open?

Yes. Admissions are open for session 2026–27, from Playgroup to Class 10, including the Hifz Department.

Which classes do you offer?

Playgroup to Class 10: Early Years, Primary, Middle and Secondary, plus Hifz.

What documents are needed?

Child’s B-Form, two passport-size photographs, a copy of the parent’s CNIC, and the school leaving certificate or last result card, if applicable.

How do I apply?

Follow the admission process, use the online form on the Admissions page, or call +92 340 1501700.

Where do I find fee details?

Please call or ask at the school office. We share up-to-date fees directly with parents.

Visiting

Can I visit before applying?

Yes. Visit during office hours, Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM–2:00 PM.

How many campuses are there?

Four. See the campuses page.

Is transport available?

Please contact the school office about routes and availability.

Free resources

Do you offer anything free for students?

Yes: free student tools and a free C++ course in Roman Urdu.
